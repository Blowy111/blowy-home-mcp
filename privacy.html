Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Blowy Home MCP is a private integration used by one household to connect its own Google Home to its own AI assistant. It has no other users.

What data is accessed

With the account owner's consent, the integration can read the home's structure, rooms, devices, device states and event history through the Google Home APIs, and send device commands the owner approves.

How data is used

The data is used only to answer the owner's own questions about their home and to carry out actions they request. It is not sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or used for advertising.

Storage

The integration keeps no database of its own. Data passes between Google and the owner's AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) under those services' own privacy policies.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Home app or in the Google Account's third-party connections settings.
